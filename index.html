Essential Workplace Quick Reference

Key information every new employee needs to know on Day 1

🕒

Office & Shift Timings

  • Office Open: 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM (Mon – Sat)
  • Holidays: 1st & 3rd Saturday + Sundays
  • First Shift: 8:50 AM – 5:50 PM (Lunch: 1:05 PM)
  • Second Shift: 9:20 AM – 6:30 PM (Lunch: 1:50 PM)
  • Night Shift: 9:00 PM – 6:00 AM (Lunch: 10:00 PM)
  • Grace Time: 10 mins (Late-in requires portal approval)
🏖

Leaves & PTO Rules

  • Annual Allotment: 18 Paid Leaves (12 Casual + 6 Sick)
  • Accrual: Credited monthly to your portal account
  • Application: Apply in HR Portal ≥ 2 days in advance
  • Sick Leave: Can be applied on the day of illness
  • Other Types: Half-Day, LOP, Maternity & Paternity
💻

IT & Role Credentials

  • Hardware: Allocated laptop, charger, and ID badge
  • Access: Role-based access (Developers get Git, VPN & Jira)
  • Security: Change temporary password within 24 hours
  • Confidentiality: Never share logins or copy company data
  • IT Support: Located on Floor 3 for technical assistance
🏢

Building Navigation

  • Top Floor: Cafeteria, dining & pantry areas
  • Washrooms: Right corner of the top-floor cafeteria
  • Floor 2: HR Support & Employee work bays
  • Floor 3: IT Support, Development & Manager cabins
  • Floor 4: Design studio & executive suites
  • Basement: B1 for two-wheelers | B2 for cars
